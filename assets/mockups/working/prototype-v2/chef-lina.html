Meet Chef Lina

From Heidelberg to New Orleans—and Back to Build Something of Her Own

Lina's is a chef-led mobile kitchen founded by Lebohang Magdalina Moeketsi, professionally known as Chef Lina. Her story is shaped by determination, professional growth and a passion for bringing people together through food.

  1. 01

    Building the foundations of her craft

    Chef Lina transformed her passion into a profession at Capsicum Culinary Studio in Sandton, completing formal culinary training and earning qualifications recognised by City & Guilds and CATHSSETA.

    Her training and practical experience equipped her with the technical knowledge, discipline and versatility required in professional kitchens—from food preparation and pastry to sautéing, grilling, plating and service.

  2. 02

    Growing through professional kitchens

    Chef Lina developed her craft at establishments including Morning Glory Café, Peeping Tom, The Houghton Hotel and Truffles on the Park.

    With every opportunity, she broadened her abilities and learned to deliver with precision, consistency and confidence in demanding kitchen environments.

  3. 03

    Taking her experience international

    Her journey later took her to New Orleans, Louisiana, where she joined Hufft Marchand Hospitality.

    Working across sauté, grill, plating and service coordination strengthened her expertise and ability to perform under pressure. It also inspired a bigger ambition: to return home and build something of her own.

  4. 04

    Returning home with a bigger vision

    Chef Lina returned to South Africa with the experience, confidence and discipline to turn her vision into a business.

    Today, Lina's is a growing mobile kitchen based in Heidelberg, offering freshly prepared meals and catering for private celebrations, corporate functions and community events.

    More than a place to buy a meal, Lina's is the next chapter in Chef Lina's journey—a proudly local business shaped by professional expertise, international experience and a genuine love for serving people through food.
