How the enquiry process works

  1. 01

    Share your event details

    Provide the occasion, date, location, estimated guest count and catering requirements.

  2. 02

    Lina reviews your request

    The team reviews the information and contacts you if any further details are required.

  3. 03

    Receive a quotation

    Lina's prepares and shares a quotation based on the confirmed requirements.

  4. 04

    Confirm the booking

    The booking is confirmed after the requirements and payment arrangements have been agreed.

What to have ready

  • Type of occasion
  • Event date and serving time
  • Venue or location
  • Estimated number of guests
  • Catering requirements
  • Dietary requirements or allergies
  • Budget range, if known
  • Special requests or additional notes
