This is a business platform, not only a website

A website shows information. A platform captures a real customer, hands that lead to Lina in a usable form, and shows her the business's overall performance. Here's how that works end to end, and how to see it live in this demo.

The customer's experience

Discover Lina's→ Browse catering or menu→ Request a quote→ Receive confirmation→ Lina follows up→ Customer receives quote→ Booking confirmed

What happens on Lina's side of the same enquiry

New enquiry→ Stored in the system→ Lina notified→ Lead reviewed→ Status updated→ Follow-up recorded→ Quote sent→ Booking confirmed or lost→ Dashboard updated

Why this matters for the business

  • No enquiry gets lost in a WhatsApp thread or a missed call — every one lands in one place.
  • Lina can see, at a glance, exactly how many leads are new, being followed up, quoted, or won.
  • Every enquiry records where it came from (Instagram, WhatsApp, website, referral) — so marketing spend can be pointed at what's actually working.
  • The dashboard turns individual enquiries into one performance picture, which is what makes this a growth platform rather than a brochure.
