# Direction A — Cinematic Chef Story (v1, 20260728)

Focus: chef identity, trailer presence, personal storytelling, premium catering credibility, editorial pacing. Built on the shared foundation in `assets/brand/working/lina-design-system-foundation-v1-20260728.md`.

## Rationale
The one confirmed genuine photograph — Chef Lina inside her trailer, "CHEF LINA" signage behind her — is a strong, human, specific image. Direction A leans on that specificity rather than volume: it treats the one real asset as the emotional anchor of the whole page instead of hiding its thinness behind stock imagery.

## One-page structure (desktop & mobile)

1. **Cinematic hero** — Full-bleed, `21:9` crop of `instagram_1785242438335.png` (chef in trailer), red duotone scrim at the base for text legibility. Positioning statement overlays in the editorial serif: short, human, not a marketing slogan (e.g. "Cooked by hand, served from the trailer" — placeholder copy, real line to be approved by Tebogo, not invented as final). Primary CTA: "See today's menu." Secondary: "Enquire for an event."
2. **Lina positioning statement** — Short editorial paragraph, generous whitespace, `--paper` background, large serif pull-quote treatment. Content sourced only from confirmed Master Brief facts (mobile kitchen + private catering, chef-led).
3. **Mobile kitchen & catering overview** — Two-column editorial split (image left, text right on desktop; stacked on mobile): left = placeholder frame labelled "Trailer exterior photo pending" (no real exterior photo confirmed yet), right = the two service modes (mobile-kitchen orders vs private catering) as short editorial copy, not feature-card grids.
4. **Interactive visual menu** — See `assets/menu/working/lina-interactive-menu-direction-v1-20260728.md`. In Direction A this section is deliberately quieter/smaller than in B or C — presented as "what's cooking" rather than a full ordering surface, in keeping with the story-led pacing.
5. **Chef & trailer story** — The largest section in this direction. Full-width `4:5` portrait crop of the confirmed chef photo, paired with editorial copy about the chef's background — using only labelled-placeholder biographical copy until Tebogo supplies real facts (I-005 in the Client Inputs Register). A second full-bleed moment reserved for the strongest reviewed video clip (pending the visual scrub noted in the Asset Register) — provisionally `VID_20260728_040854_122.mp4`, the longest continuous clip, muted autoplay loop.
6. **Catering occasions & services** — Editorial list (not icon cards) of the four occasions from the Master Brief (weddings, funerals, corporate, private functions), set as large serif labels with short supporting lines, image-free to keep pacing calm after the media-heavy chef section.
7. **Gallery/image sequence** — Expanding lightbox gallery. Currently populated with the one confirmed photo plus clearly labelled "more photos coming soon" placeholders — the register shows why (8 of 12 supplied images were unrelated fine-dining content and were rejected).
8. **Order or catering enquiry** — Two distinct, clearly separated actions per the foundation's form behaviour: catering enquiry form (Journey A) and WhatsApp-assisted mobile-kitchen order (Journey B). POPIA consent required on the form.
9. **Contact & social** — Address, hours, WhatsApp, Instagram — all fields sourced from the Client Inputs Register; anything not yet confirmed (operating hours I-007, service area I-008) shows a clearly labelled "confirming soon" state, not an invented value.

## Motion & pacing
Slower than B or C — longer holds on the hero and chef story sections, generous scroll distance between reveals. This direction earns its "cinematic" label through restraint, not through more animation.

## Strengths
- Honest, human, differentiated — a chef-led food truck telling its own story rather than looking like a generic restaurant template.
- Makes the single strong asset do maximum work.
- Naturally extensible once more chef/BTS photography is confirmed.

## Risks
- Heaviest reliance on the thinnest part of the current asset library (chef/trailer imagery) — if Tebogo cannot supply more chef-facing photography, sections 3 and 7 stay placeholder-heavy longer than the other directions.
- Menu/ordering is deliberately de-emphasised, which slightly under-serves the mobile-kitchen quick-order journey (Journey B) relative to Direction B/C.
