# Lina Digital Growth Platform — Master Product & Delivery Brief
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 28 July 2026  
**Owner:** Tebogo Ngingi  
**Delivery brands:** Apprigate (platform) and Kasi Kanvas (branding, creative and growth support)

## 1. Purpose
Create a client-ready digital growth platform and supporting brand-application system for Lina's mobile-kitchen and private-catering business. The product must help the business attract customers, convert enquiries and orders, manage follow-up, present a professional image and measure sales activity.

This is not a brochure website. It is a controlled Phase 1 business platform with a clear path to future expansion.

## 2. Known business context
- The client is a professional chef.
- The business operates a mobile kitchen and provides private catering.
- Key occasions include weddings, funerals, corporate events and other private functions.
- The business has an existing logo.
- The business has an Instagram presence with possible interim imagery.
- The client will provide a menu.
- Stronger original photography will be produced later.
- The programme must include marketing, sales support and performance tracking.
- Three product/design samples must be available for the client discussion on 29 July 2026.
- The intended Phase 1 completion date is 31 July 2026.

## 3. Business outcomes
The Phase 1 product should enable Lina to:
1. Present a professional and coherent catering brand.
2. Generate catering enquiries and meal orders.
3. Guide customers through a simple, mobile-first journey.
4. Capture and store every lead reliably.
5. Track enquiry/order status and next actions.
6. Promote offers through WhatsApp, Instagram and other digital channels.
7. Demonstrate progress and performance to GEP or other support stakeholders.
8. Establish reusable assets for ongoing sales and marketing.

## 4. Primary users
### Customer
A person or organisation looking for catering, meals or event food services.

### Business administrator
Lina or an authorised assistant managing menu items, enquiries, orders, follow-ups and content.

### Programme/review stakeholder
A GEP or support-programme representative reviewing implementation, marketing activity and measurable progress.

## 5. Core customer journeys
### Journey A — Private catering enquiry
Discover business → understand catering services → view examples/packages/menu → choose occasion → submit event details → receive acknowledgement → Lina reviews lead → quotation/follow-up → accepted/declined → post-event follow-up.

### Journey B — Mobile-kitchen order
Discover menu/special → choose item → select collection/delivery or WhatsApp ordering route → provide contact/order details → receive confirmation → Lina prepares/updates status → customer receives collection/delivery information.

### Journey C — Social-to-platform conversion
See Instagram/WhatsApp promotion → open campaign link → land on matching offer → enquire/order → source is recorded → Lina follows up → performance appears in dashboard.

### Journey D — Repeat customer
Return via saved link/WhatsApp → reorder or request catering → details are recognised where appropriate → Lina sees previous interaction → faster follow-up.

## 6. Phase 1 product scope
### Public experience
- Homepage with strong value proposition and primary calls to action.
- Catering/services page.
- Menu or featured-items experience.
- Event/catering enquiry form.
- Order or WhatsApp-assisted ordering flow.
- About/chef story section using approved information.
- Gallery using approved Instagram or supplied media.
- Contact, operating hours, service area and social links.
- Privacy notice and POPIA consent.

### Admin and operations
- Secure admin access or controlled admin route.
- Lead/order inbox.
- Statuses: New, Contacted, Quoted/Confirmed, In Progress, Completed, Lost/Cancelled.
- Notes and next-action field.
- Source tracking: website, Instagram, WhatsApp, referral, other.
- Basic menu/content management where feasible.
- Basic dashboard: enquiries/orders, status breakdown, popular offers/items, sources and simple date filtering.

### Marketing and sales support
- Digital flyer template.
- Social post/story templates.
- Digital business card/contact card.
- Menu presentation template.
- Campaign landing-page pattern.
- Initial 30-day marketing and sales action plan.

## 7. Three client-review directions
These are three presentation directions, not three full builds.

### Direction 1 — Premium Catering
Emphasis: weddings, funerals, corporate and private-event catering; trust, presentation, packages and quotation enquiries.

### Direction 2 — Mobile Kitchen & Ordering
Emphasis: menu discovery, daily specials, collection/delivery and fast WhatsApp-assisted conversion.

### Direction 3 — Integrated Growth Platform
Emphasis: catering + mobile kitchen + lead/order tracking + campaign measurement. This is the recommended Apprigate direction.

Each direction should include: hero treatment, homepage structure, one key conversion screen, mobile preview and a short rationale.

## 8. Visual and brand system
The existing logo remains the starting brand asset. Before full interface production, establish:
- Approved logo usage and clear space.
- Colour palette derived from or compatible with the logo.
- Primary and secondary typography.
- Button and CTA hierarchy.
- Cards, form fields, navigation and spacing rules.
- Photography treatment for interim Instagram imagery.
- Icon and illustration approach.
- Mobile-first component behaviour.

No final brand redesign is included unless separately approved. Brand application and refinement are included.

## 9. Branding asset milestone
Branding assets must live inside the project, not across chats or random folders.

Required asset outputs:
- Brand application sheet.
- Digital business card.
- Letterhead/quotation header.
- Menu design/template.
- Social post and story templates.
- Promotional flyer.
- Uniform mock-up.
- Mobile-kitchen/vehicle or service mock-up where source images allow.
- Outdoor sign/banner mock-up.
- Website/device mock-ups for client presentation.

All source files, approved exports and mock-ups must be stored under `/assets` using the structure defined in `docs/LINA-ASSET-REGISTER.md`.

## 10. Non-functional requirements
- Responsive mobile-first design.
- Fast, accessible and understandable interaction.
- Production-capable persistent data storage.
- Form validation and clear error/success states.
- Role/access protection for admin data.
- POPIA-aligned consent and privacy information.
- No critical data stored only in localStorage.
- Error logging and backup/export approach.
- Deployable private preview before client review.

## 11. Scope control
Once the client chooses a direction, Phase 1 scope is frozen. New requests are recorded as:
- **Critical for Phase 1** — necessary for the core journey or contractual outcome.
- **Phase 2** — valuable but not required for 31 July.
- **Rejected/unnecessary** — does not support the agreed outcome.

A new Phase 1 item must replace another item or receive an explicit delivery-impact decision.

## 12. Content policy
Missing content does not stop production. Use labelled placeholders, maintain the Client Inputs Register and replace content when supplied. Do not invent facts, customer claims, prices or testimonials.

## 13. Success criteria
Phase 1 succeeds when:
- The client can understand and choose from three strong directions.
- One coherent direction is implemented.
- The core customer journeys work end-to-end.
- Leads/orders are stored and visible to the business.
- Mobile experience is polished.
- Branding and digital sales assets are organised and presentation-ready.
- Tests and production build pass.
- Outstanding inputs and Phase 2 items are documented.

## 14. Brand responsibility split
- **Apprigate:** platform architecture, customer journeys, website/backend, dashboard, lead/order management, hosting and Care.
- **Kasi Kanvas:** brand application, mock-ups, digital stationery, social templates, campaign assets and marketing support.
